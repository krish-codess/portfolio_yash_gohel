**Section framing (live):** *Flagship · TatvaCare / Zydus Wellness.*

### Built a DTC health brand's entire storefront from an empty page

**Hook:** GoodFlip wasn't selling a supplement. It was selling doctors, diagnostics, devices, coaching and nutrition — and making all of it simple enough to act on in one sitting. I built the place that sold it: Shopify, Amazon, Flipkart, copy, creative and checkout included.

**Live:** shop.goodflip.in

**Numbers:**

- **3 platforms** live from zero — Shopify, Amazon, Flipkart
- **10+** product pages where I wrote the words and designed the page they sit on
- **~15%** AOV lift, from upsell and cross-sell in the cart *(Q1)*
- **~28%** organic traffic growth in three months
- **~18%** CPA cut, from landing pages matched to ad intent

**Brief:** Turn a complicated health proposition into a multi-platform business, fast, with no storefront to start from.

**The argument I made:** A product page isn't copy dropped onto a template — it's one object. The sentence and the layout have to be built together or the buyer stalls. So I didn't hand copy to a designer. I built both.

**My role:** End-to-end owner — strategy and naming, identity and packaging, store architecture through checkout, the Amazon A+ and brand store, the Flipkart listings, 10+ films, paid and organic social, and the CRM journeys that keep people after the sale.

**What I learned (keep — it's honest and good):** The hardest part wasn't the store. It was making a genuinely complicated health proposition feel simple enough for someone to act on in one sitting.

**Tools:** Shopify · Amazon Seller Central · Flipkart · Figma · Photoshop · Illustrator
