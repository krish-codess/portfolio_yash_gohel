Context & approach

Context & role

GoodFlip started with no storefront. I took it from an idea to a live, multi-platform DTC business.

My role: End-to-end owner — store architecture, content, creative, and conversion.

Result: A live, multi-channel DTC store generating revenue across three platforms, with measurable lifts in AOV, organic traffic, and CPA.

What I did

  • Built the full Shopify store: architecture, theme, navigation taxonomy, collection structure, checkout flow.
  • Wrote and designed 10+ PDPs where copy and visuals were built together to guide the buyer.
  • Set up and optimized Amazon (A+ content, brand store) and Flipkart listings.
  • Integrated upsell/cross-sell into the cart to lift AOV.
  • Built campaign landing pages matched to paid-ad intent, cutting CPA ~18%.
