Context & approach

Context & role

Streak's trading platform served lakhs of first-time retail traders who found the product's language — and the market itself — intimidating. There was no structured education layer and no consistent product voice.

My role: Content strategist — owned the Help Centre build and all in-product UX copy.

What I did

  • Wrote an entire trading-education Help Centre — strategic trading, technical indicators, strategies — with 100+ articles and instructional videos, from nothing.
  • Authored the full UI/UX copy for the platform: onboarding, in-product flows, microcopy — turning jargon into clear, confident language.
  • Conducted 150+ user interviews and synthesized 1,000+ responses to shape content strategy, user journey, and product direction.
  • Led growth partnerships and co-marketing/B2B integrations bringing in 5,000+ new users monthly.
