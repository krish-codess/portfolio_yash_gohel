How I work

Strategy first. Systems second.
AI where it actually helps.

I use AI to compress the repetitive parts of marketing: research, segmentation, ideation, production and analysis, so more time goes into the parts that require judgement.

01 · Start with the problem

I don't start with ‘What should we post?’ I start with: what are we trying to change?

02 · Make the strategy visible

A strategy isn't useful if the creative team can't turn it into something. I translate positioning into campaigns, content systems and executable briefs.

03 · Ship

I'd rather test a strong idea this week than debate a perfect one for three weeks.

04 · Follow the data

Clicks tell me what attracted attention. Conversions tell me whether the idea actually mattered.

05 · Automate the boring

If AI can do the repetitive 80%, I'll let it. The remaining 20% is where judgement lives.

Proof, not description

Strategy → design → code → iteration → shipped, using the same process described above.

The problem

The platform served lakhs of first-time retail traders who found the language intimidating: there was no structured education layer and no consistent product voice.

My role

Content strategist. Owned the Help Centre build and all in-product UX copy.

The execution
  • Wrote the trading-education Help Centre: 100+ articles & videos, from nothing.
  • Authored the full UI/UX copy: onboarding, in-product flows, and microcopy, turning jargon into clarity.
  • Conducted 150+ user interviews and synthesised 1,000+ responses.
  • Led growth partnerships & co-marketing bringing 5,000+ new users/month.
What I learned

Financial confidence is a language problem before it’s a product problem. Most first-time traders don’t drop off because the platform is hard to use, they drop off because the terminology makes them feel like they don’t belong yet.

About

I was never very good at staying in one box.

I like ideas, but I also want to know whether they worked. I like brand, but I want to understand the funnel behind it. I like making things, which is probably why I've ended up directing shoots, writing campaigns, building stores, designing landing pages and obsessing over dashboards.
